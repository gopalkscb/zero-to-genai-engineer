# Discount Policy

## Manager limits (v1.2)

Restaurant managers may apply a discount of up to 15% on the order subtotal without further approval. Discounts between 16% and 25% require approval from the shift supervisor. Any discount above 25% must be authorised by the owner in writing. Every discount must be recorded on the bill with a reason code (promotion, complaint, staff meal, partner offer).

## Promotions (v1.2)

Standard promotional discounts are 5%, 10% and 15%. Promotions cannot be combined with loyalty rewards on the same bill. Complimentary items are not treated as discounts and must be recorded separately as a comp line with manager initials.

## Weekday promotions (v1.3)

From Monday to Thursday only, the restaurant runs a **Weekday Lunch Promotion**: 10% off all Main Course items ordered between 14:00 and 17:00 (Asia/Dubai time). The discount applies to food subtotal only — not beverages, desserts, or delivery fees. It does not stack with other coupons or the loyalty programme. Display the offer on table tents and the POS as **WEEKDAY10**.

## Weekend and public-holiday rules (v1.3)

Fridays and Saturdays are peak service days. Automatic weekday lunch pricing must **not** apply on weekends. On UAE public holidays when the restaurant is open, only pre-approved holiday campaigns (owner sign-off) may run — default weekday promotions are suspended.

## Corporate and group bookings (v1.2)

Groups of 10 or more guests may receive a fixed 12% discount on the food subtotal when booked at least 48 hours in advance. A 50% deposit is required for groups above 20. Corporate accounts with a signed agreement may use invoice billing; those discounts are negotiated separately and are not entered as ad-hoc manager discounts.
