# Patio and Seating Policy

## Indoor vs outdoor (v1.0)

The restaurant has 20 indoor tables and 8 patio tables. Patio service is available October through April when daytime highs stay below 38°C. During summer months (May–September), patio seating is closed unless the owner announces a special evening event.

## Weather decisions (v1.1)

If the forecast shows rain probability above 40% or wind above 35 km/h during service hours, the shift manager closes the patio and moves reservations indoors where possible. Fans and misting are used when temperature is 32°C–38°C and humidity is moderate — not during sandstorms or extreme heat advisories.

## Table allocation (v1.0)

Parties of 1–2: indoor tables 1–8 preferred. Parties of 3–4: indoor or patio. Parties of 5+: combine tables 18–20 or patio 6–8 with 24-hour notice. Do not seat incomplete large parties more than 15 minutes before the full group arrives during peak hours.

## Accessibility (v1.0)

Table 1 near the entrance is reserved for wheelchair access when requested. Keep that path clear of high chairs and waiting guests. Restrooms are indoor only; assist patio guests through the side door during staffed hours.
