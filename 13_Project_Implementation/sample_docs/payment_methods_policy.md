# Payment Methods Policy

## Accepted methods (v1.0)

The restaurant accepts **card**, **cash**, **UPI**, and **wallet** payments. Card is preferred for orders above AED 200. All methods must reconcile to the payments table with status **paid** before an order counts as revenue. Failed terminal transactions must be retried or switched to another method — do not mark the order paid manually without supervisor approval.

## Cash handling (v1.1)

Maximum AED 500 cash in the float at opening. Drops to the safe every AED 1,500 collected. Two staff must witness safe drops and sign the cash log. Give change only from the official float — personal money must not enter the till.

## Tips and service charge (v1.0)

A 10% optional service charge may be added to dine-in bills for parties of 6 or more when announced on the menu footer. Tips on card are pooled and distributed weekly among front-of-house staff. Kitchen tips from the shared jar are counted every Sunday evening.

## VAT and receipts (v1.0)

All prices on the menu are exclusive of 5% VAT unless marked otherwise. Receipts must show TRN (tax registration number), order ID, and itemised lines. Corporate invoices require the guest's company TRN entered at checkout before the end of the business day.
