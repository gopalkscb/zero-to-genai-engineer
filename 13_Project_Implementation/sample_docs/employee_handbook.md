# Employee Handbook

## Shift timings (v3.1)

The restaurant operates two shifts. The morning shift runs from 10:00 to 16:00 and the evening shift from 16:00 to 24:00. Staff are entitled to a 30-minute break per shift and a minimum of 11 hours rest between shifts. Kitchen prep for lunch service begins at 10:30; the dining room opens to guests at 12:00.

## Leave (v3.1)

Employees accrue two days of paid leave per month. Leave requests must be submitted at least seven days in advance except in emergencies. No more than two kitchen staff may be on leave on the same day. During Ramadan, reduced hours must be agreed with the manager at least two weeks ahead.

## Roles and uniforms (v3.2)

Front-of-house staff wear the branded black shirt and name badge. Kitchen staff wear non-slip shoes, hair nets, and aprons at all times on the line. Managers on floor duty must be reachable on the house WhatsApp group during service hours. Only trained staff may operate the tandoor or deep fryer.

## Guest complaints escalation (v3.2)

Servers should attempt to resolve minor complaints (wait time, wrong garnish) without a discount. If a guest asks to speak to a manager, the shift manager must attend within three minutes. Document the complaint in the daily log with order ID and resolution. Offer refunds only within the limits of the Refund Policy — do not promise exceptions on behalf of the owner.
