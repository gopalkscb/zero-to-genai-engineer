# Food Safety SOP

## Storage (v1.0)

Raw meat must be stored below 4 degrees Celsius and kept separate from vegetables and cooked food. Every storage container must be labelled with the date of receipt. Stock is rotated on a first-in first-out basis. The walk-in cooler temperature log must be checked and signed every four hours during service.

## Allergens (v1.0)

Common allergens on the menu include dairy, nuts and shellfish. Dishes containing paneer, butter, cream or milk carry dairy. Prawn Koliwada and Fish Curry contain shellfish or fish. Staff must inform guests of allergens on request and never guarantee a completely nut-free kitchen because shared fryers are used for some starters.

## Cross-contamination (v1.1)

Use colour-coded boards: red for raw meat, green for vegetables, blue for cooked protein. Wash hands and change gloves when switching from raw to ready-to-eat tasks. Separate tongs must be used for veg and non-veg tandoor service. Any dropped utensil on the floor must be washed and sanitised before reuse.

## Temperature and holding (v1.1)

Hot-held curries must stay above 63°C. Do not reheat rice more than once. Biryani pots leaving the kitchen must be covered and time-stamped. Any item in the danger zone (5°C–63°C) for more than two hours must be discarded and logged in the waste sheet.

## Cleaning schedule (v1.0)

Deep clean fryers every Monday after lunch close. Sanitise all food-contact surfaces every two hours during service. Pest-control visits are scheduled monthly; report droppings or gnaw marks to the manager immediately.
