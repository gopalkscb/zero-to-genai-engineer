# Delivery and Takeaway Policy

## Order types (v1.0)

The POS supports three order types: **dine_in**, **takeaway**, and **delivery**. Revenue analytics treat all paid orders equally unless a report explicitly filters by `order_type`. Delivery orders incur a flat AED 8 delivery fee added after tax on the guest receipt (not part of food subtotal for discount calculations).

## Delivery radius and timing (v1.1)

Delivery is available within 8 km of the restaurant. Quoted delivery time is 35–50 minutes depending on traffic. Orders above AED 150 qualify for free delivery on weekdays only. The dispatcher must confirm the rider pickup time in the tablet log.

## Packaging standards (v1.0)

Curries are packed in sealed leak-proof containers with separate compartments for rice and bread when requested. Hot items must leave the pass at least 60°C; use insulated bags for rides longer than 20 minutes. Include napkins, spoons, and allergen card for every delivery.

## Takeaway handoff (v1.0)

Takeaway orders are collected from the front counter. Verify the order name and last four digits of the phone number before release. Paid takeaway orders cannot be modified after the kitchen ticket fires — guest must place a new order for additions.
