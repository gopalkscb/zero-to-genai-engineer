# Refund Policy

## Eligibility (v2.0)

A full refund is issued when an order is cancelled before preparation begins. Once food preparation has started, only a partial refund covering undelivered items is permitted. Quality complaints are refunded at the manager's discretion up to the value of the affected items. Refunds are never issued for change-of-mind after food has been served unless there is a documented food-safety concern.

## Method (v2.0)

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds may take three to five business days. Cash refunds are given immediately from the till and must be logged in the audit record with the manager's name. UPI and wallet refunds are processed through the same terminal used for the original payment within 24 hours.

## Delivery and takeaway refunds (v2.1)

For **delivery** orders, a full refund is available if the rider has not left the restaurant. If the order is late by more than 45 minutes beyond the quoted time, the manager may offer a partial refund up to 20% of the order total or a complimentary dessert — not both. **Takeaway** orders cancelled at the counter before the ticket is sent to the kitchen receive a full refund automatically.

## Disputed card charges (v2.0)

If a guest disputes a card charge with their bank, do not issue a duplicate cash refund. Escalate to the owner and attach the receipt, order ID, and payment reference from the POS. All chargeback responses must be filed within five business days.
