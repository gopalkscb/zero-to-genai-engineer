# Opening Hours Policy

## Service hours (v1.0)

The restaurant is open daily from 12:00 noon to 23:00. Last orders for the kitchen are taken at 22:30. The restaurant remains closed on the first of January each year. Beverages and desserts may be served until 23:00 but no new kitchen tickets after 22:30.

## Ramadan hours (v1.1)

During Ramadan, opening hours shift to 18:00–01:00 for dine-in. A limited iftar set menu is offered from 18:00 to 20:30. Staff breaks are scheduled around prayer times; see the Ramadan roster posted in the staff room each week.

## Early closure and emergencies (v1.0)

The manager may close early only for safety (power failure, flooding, health inspection hold). Post a notice on the door and update Google Business Profile within 30 minutes. Partial refunds for prepaid bookings follow the Refund Policy.

## Reservation slots (v1.1)

Peak slots are 19:00–21:00 Thursday through Saturday. Walk-ins are accepted but wait times may exceed 30 minutes on weekends. Reservations held for 15 minutes; after that the table is released.
