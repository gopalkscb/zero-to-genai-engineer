# Loyalty Program

## Points earning (v1.0)

Registered guests earn 1 point per AED 10 spent on food (excluding tax, delivery fee, and tips). Points post within 24 hours of a **paid** order. Cancelled or fully refunded orders do not earn points. Staff meals and employee discounts do not earn points.

## Redemption rules (v1.0)

100 points = AED 10 off the food subtotal. Redemption requires a minimum order of AED 50. Points cannot be redeemed on public holidays or combined with weekday lunch promotions (WEEKDAY10). Maximum one redemption per bill.

## Enrollment and privacy (v1.1)

Sign-up is via phone number at the POS or QR code on the table tent. Do not share guest phone numbers with third-party marketers. Guests may request deletion of their profile by emailing the owner; completed deletion removes unused points without cash compensation.

## Manager overrides (v1.0)

Managers may grant a one-time 50-point bonus for documented service recovery (long wait, wrong order corrected). Document the order ID in the loyalty override log. Do not manually add points to personal or family accounts.
